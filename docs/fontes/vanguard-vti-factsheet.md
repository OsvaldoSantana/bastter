## Aviso de mudança de nome (texto literal, relevante para rastreabilidade futura)

"Vanguard recently announced upcoming changes to the names of multiple Vanguard U.S. equity index funds. Following Morningstar's recent acquisition of CRSP, the benchmarks tracked by these index funds will be rebranded from CRSP to Morningstar. In addition, Vanguard is adding 'Morningstar' to the names of the funds tracking the Morningstar Indexes. For example, Vanguard Total Stock Market Index Fund will become Vanguard Morningstar Total Stock Market Index Fund. The name changes for both the Vanguard funds and the benchmarks are expected to occur in July 2026."
